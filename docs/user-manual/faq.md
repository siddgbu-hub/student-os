# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about using Student OS on Web and Android.

---

### 1. How do I start studying with the timer?
1. Go to the **Study** module.
2. Select your **Subject** and optionally a **Chapter**.
3. Click **Start Study Session**.
4. The stopwatch timer starts immediately. When you finish, click **Complete & Save** to record your study time.

---

### 2. Can I pause a study session if I get interrupted?
Yes. Click **Pause Session** on the timer card. The timer pauses and changes color. When you return to your desk, click **Resume Session**. If you are on Android, you can also tap Pause directly in your phone's notification bar.

---

### 3. Where can I see my past study history?
- **Today's History**: Visible under the **Today's Focus Summary** section at the bottom of the **Study** page.
- **Historical Trends**: Check the **Analytics** module for complete daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly study summaries.
- **Consistency Calendar**: View the **16-Week Activity Heatmap** at the bottom of your **Dashboard**.

---

### 4. How do I create a daily planner task?
1. Go to the **Planner** page.
2. Click **+ New Study Block** (top right).
3. Enter your task title, choose an associated subject, set an estimated duration, and select a priority (**High**, **Medium**, or **Low**).
4. Click **Create Study Block**.

---

### 5. How does the Spaced Repetition Revision Tracker work?
When you add a revision topic, Student OS automatically creates review checkpoints at increasing time intervals (1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 21 days). Reviewing items when they appear in your **Due Today** queue prevents memory decay and maintains a high **Retention Score**.

---

### 6. Can I use Student OS on both my laptop and my Android phone?
Yes. Student OS is built for seamless cross-platform use. You can plan your schedule on your laptop web browser ([`studentos.kryvlance.in`](https://studentos.kryvlance.in)) and run your desk timers or check off tasks on your Android phone using the APK.

---

### 7. What happens when my 7-day free trial ends?
Your data is **100% safe and never deleted**. Your Dashboard and Account settings remain open. Active operational modules (starting new timers, creating new tasks) will pause until you upgrade to Student OS Pro.

### 9. How do I switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode?
Go to **Account & Settings** > **Appearance Preferences** and select **Dark Mode**, **Light Mode**, or **System Default**.

---

### 10. How do I sign out of my account?
Click the **Sign Out** button in the top navigation bar on desktop, or scroll to the bottom of the **Account** tab on mobile.
