# What Happens When Your Trial Expires

If your 7-day free trial concludes before you upgrade to Student OS Pro, your account transitions gracefully into a protected state. Student OS prioritizes **student data safety**—your hard work is never deleted or discarded.

---

## 1. What Remains Fully Accessible

When your trial expires, you will not be locked out of the application. You can still:

- ✅ **Access the Dashboard**: View your current exam goal countdown, syllabus completion percentage, and historical streak.
- ✅ **Access Account & Settings**: Update your profile, adjust themes (Dark/Light), manage authenticated devices, and sign in or out.
- ✅ **Review Your Existing Data**: Your recorded study minutes, subjects, and completed chapters remain safe in your cloud account.

---

## 2. What Becomes Protected (Pro Features)

Active operational modules will display a friendly **Pro Access Required** banner:

- 🔒 **Study Engine**: Starting new timer sessions and creating new subjects/chapters requires an active Pro subscription.
- 🔒 **Academic Planner**: Adding new daily study blocks or modifying scheduled tasks is paused.
- 🔒 **Revision Tracker**: Starting new spaced repetition sessions is locked.
- 🔒 **Detailed Analytics**: Historical multi-month chart drill-downs are paused.

## 4. How to Reactivate Full Access

Reactivating full access takes less than 2 minutes:

1. Click **Upgrade Now** on any locked screen banner or open **Account > Subscription**.
2. Choose either the **Yearly Plan (₹2,499)** or **Monthly Plan (₹299)**.
3. Complete the quick WhatsApp activation.
4. Your account is instantly refreshed, and you can immediately resume your study sessions where you left off.
