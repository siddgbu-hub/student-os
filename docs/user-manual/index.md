# Student OS User Manual

Welcome to the official **Student OS User Manual & Documentation**. Student OS is an integrated academic productivity operating system engineered to help students structure their preparation, track active study hours, implement spaced repetition revision, organize daily tasks, and achieve their target examination goals.

This user manual covers both the **Student OS Web Application** and the **Student OS Android Mobile App (APK)**.

---

## 1. Welcome to Student OS

### What is Student OS?
Student OS is a personal academic management system designed to eliminate fragmentation in student workflows. Instead of juggling separate apps for timers, to-do lists, calendars, and revision reminders, Student OS unifies your entire study routine into a cohesive ecosystem.


---

### What Problems Does Student OS Solve?

1. **Unstructured Study Sessions**: Prevents passive reading by timing active focus sessions linked directly to specific subjects and chapters.
2. **Forgetting Curve & Ineffective Revision**: Automates spaced repetition intervals so you review topics before retention fades.
3. **Overwhelmed Exam Preparation**: Breaks down huge exam syllabi into manageable daily chapter and minute targets based on your exam deadline.
4. **Scattered Planning**: Connects what you plan in your calendar with what you actually study in real time.
5. **Cross-Device Fragmentation**: Keeps your data synchronized across your laptop/desktop browser and your Android smartphone.
