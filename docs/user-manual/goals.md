# Goals & Exam Preparation

Student OS includes a dedicated **Goal Engine** built specifically for students preparing for competitive exams, board exams, university semester finals, and professional certifications.

Instead of vague motivation, the goal engine provides mathematical clarity by converting large syllabi into precise daily targets based on your exact exam date.

---

## 1. Setting Up an Exam Goal

You can configure your target exam from either the **Dashboard** (by clicking **Set Exam Goal** or **Edit Goal**) or through the **Planner** page.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          SET EXAM TARGET GOAL                          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Exam Title:            [ UPSC Civil Services Exam 2026               ] │
│ Target Exam Date:      [ 2026-05-24                                  ] │
│ Total Syllabus Chapters: [ 120                                       ] │
│                                                                        │
│                      [ Cancel ]   [ Save Exam Goal ]                   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Configuration Fields
1. **Exam Title**: Name of your target examination or academic milestone.
2. **Target Exam Date**: The scheduled date of your examination.
3. **Total Syllabus Chapters**: Total number of chapters or units across all subjects you need to cover.

---

## 2. Dynamic Pace & Trajectory Calculations

Once your goal is saved, Student OS dynamically calculates your preparation trajectory in real time:

| Calculated Metric | What it Tells You |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Days / Weeks Remaining** | A real-time countdown to your target exam date. |
| **Completion Percentage** | Ratio of finished chapters divided by total syllabus chapters (`Completed / Total * 100`). |
| **Required Daily Pace** | The average focus minutes you must study every day to complete your syllabus before exam day. |
| **Chapters / Day Velocity** | The average chapter completion rate needed per day (e.g., *0.4 Chapters/Day*). |

---

## 3. Understanding Pacing Status Badges

Your active goal displays a color-coded status badge to help you immediately gauge your progress:

- **🟢 ON TRACK**: Your current completion velocity perfectly aligns with your deadline.
- **🔵 AHEAD OF SCHEDULE**: You have completed more chapters than projected for today's date.
- **🟡 AT RISK**: Your completion pace is beginning to lag; slight increase in daily study hours recommended.
- **🔴 BEHIND SCHEDULE**: Substantial backlog detected; consider rescheduling non-essential tasks or increasing daily time blocks.
- **⚪ NOT STARTED**: Goal created, but no chapters have been marked completed yet.

---

## 4. How the Study Module Connects to Your Goal

The Goal Engine is directly linked to your syllabus:
1. When you go to the **Study** module and check the box next to any chapter, your **Completed Chapters** count increments by 1.
2. Your **Goal Completion Percentage** increases automatically.
3. Your **Remaining Daily Pace** recalculates to reflect the newly completed topic.

This automatic feedback loop ensures you always know exactly where you stand on your journey to exam day.
