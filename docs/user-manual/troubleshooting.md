# Troubleshooting Guide

Quick, practical solutions for common technical questions on Student OS Web and Android.

---

### 1. Verification Code (OTP) is Not Arriving in Email
- **Check Spam / Promotions Folders**: Verification emails may occasionally be filtered by Gmail or Outlook.
- **Confirm Email Spelling**: Double-check that your email address was typed correctly without typos or extra spaces.
- **Wait 60 Seconds**: Avoid spamming the request button. OTP codes are valid for 5 minutes.
- **Alternative Sign-In**: You can sign in instantly using **Continue with Google**.

---

### 2. Browser Page is Blank or Not Updating
- **Perform a Hard Refresh**: Press `Ctrl + Shift + R` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd + Shift + R` (Mac) to reload fresh assets.
- **Click "Refresh Data"**: Click the circular refresh icon in the top navigation bar to trigger a live database re-sync.
- **Clear Browser Cache**: Clear cached data for `studentos.kryvlance.in` in browser settings.

---

### 3. Study Timer Did Not Save My Time
- **Ensure You Clicked "Complete & Save"**: If you close your browser tab or shut down your computer while a timer is running without clicking *Complete & Save*, the session will be discarded to prevent inaccurate logging.
- **Keep Tab Awake on Web**: On laptops, avoid setting the active study tab into aggressive deep sleep. On Android, the timer runs safely in the background via the Foreground Service.

---

### 4. Android Status Bar Timer Notification Disappeared
- **Enable Notification Permissions**: Go to your phone's **Settings > Apps > Student OS > Notifications** and ensure notifications are set to **Allowed**.
- **Battery Optimization Settings**: Go to **Settings > Apps > Student OS > Battery** and select **Unrestricted** or **Not Optimized** to prevent Android from closing the background timer during long study blocks.

---

### 5. Multi-Device or Session Disconnect
- **Single Active Phone Policy**: Logging into Student OS on a second Android smartphone will automatically deactivate the previous phone session for security.
- **Web Sessions**: You can keep web browser tabs open on multiple computers simultaneously without disconnecting your phone.
- **Review Active Devices**: Go to **Account & Settings > Authenticated Devices** to view and revoke any unrecognized sessions.

---

### 6. Need Additional Assistance?
If you encounter an issue not covered above, reach out directly to the Student OS support team via WhatsApp or through your Account settings page.
