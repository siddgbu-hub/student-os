# Quick Start Guide

Follow this simple 8-step daily workflow to get the most out of Student OS from day one.

---

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      THE 8-STEP DAILY STUDY LOOP                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Sign In ──► 2. Set Goal ──► 3. Plan Day ──► 4. Run Study Session    │
│                                                       │                │
│ 8. Consistency ◄── 7. Review Stats ◄── 6. Revise ◄── 5. Check Chapters │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### Step 1: Sign In to Your Workspace
Open [`studentos.kryvlance.in`](https://studentos.kryvlance.in) on your computer or launch the Student OS Android app. Enter your email to receive a 6-digit OTP code, or sign in with Google SSO.

---

### Step 2: Set Your Target Exam Goal
Click **Set Exam Goal** on the Dashboard. Enter your target exam name, scheduled exam date, and total chapters. Student OS will immediately calculate your required daily study pace.

---

### Step 3: Add Your Subjects & Chapters
Navigate to the **Study** page. Click **+ Subject** to add your courses (e.g., *Mathematics*, *Physics*, *Biology*). Under each subject, click **+ Chapter** to populate your syllabus topics.

---

### Step 4: Plan Your Day in the Academic Planner
Open the **Planner** every morning. Click **+ New Study Block** to schedule your 2–4 most important tasks for the day, assigning high-priority tags to your hardest topics.

---

### Step 5: Start an Active Study Session
When sitting down to study:
1. Go to the **Study** module.
2. Select your target subject and chapter.
3. Click **Start Study Session**.
4. When finished, click **Complete & Save** to record your active focus time.

---

### Step 6: Mark Finished Chapters & Check Off Tasks
As you complete topics, check off the chapter boxes on the Study page and tick your planner tasks as done. Watch your Exam Goal progress bar increase!

---

### Step 7: Clear Your Due Spaced Revisions
Open the **Revision** module. Check your **Due Today** queue. Spend 15–30 minutes reviewing scheduled topics and click **Complete & Save** to boost your **Retention Score**.

---

### Step 8: Review Daily Analytics & Maintain Your Streak
Before wrapping up your day, check your **Dashboard** and **Analytics** pages. Celebrate hitting your target focus hours, watch your consistency heatmap illuminate, and keep your daily streak alive!
